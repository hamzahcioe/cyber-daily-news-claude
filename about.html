About and method

A daily news briefing on cybersecurity, major outages and global technology. It is reporting only: no advice, and no tailoring to any organisation.

How it is made

An automated process reads a wide set of public sources each working day, cross-checks stories against primary sources such as vendor advisories, maintainer announcements, status pages and company filings, and writes the brief. Briefs are published as static files and are never edited silently: when a story changes, a dated update is added.

Confidence labels

CONFIRMED The affected party or an authority has acknowledged it, or multiple reputable outlets have verified it.

VENDOR-ACKNOWLEDGED A vendor or company has said something, but details are thin.

UNCONFIRMED CLAIM A claim, rumour, state-media assertion or single-source report that has not been verified. Unconfirmed items are only included when they concern a major name or are corroborated by two or more independent sources, and the claim tracker records whether each is later confirmed or denied.

Severity

Critical, High, Medium, Low or Awareness. It is a general assessment based on scale, exploitation status, ease of exploitation, vendor-assigned severity and the number of people or organisations affected. It is not tailored to any organisation. Non-security tech stories are marked Awareness unless there is a significant security or market consequence.

Outlet counts

Where a story shows "covered by N outlets", the count is calculated by the process itself from the outlets it read. It is a rough signal of how widely a story is being reported, not a measure of accuracy.

Global scope and neutrality

Coverage deliberately spans regions, including China, Russia, Ukraine, the Middle East, South and East Asia, Africa and Latin America. Contested claims are attributed to whoever made them, state-run media is labelled as such, and where sides give conflicting accounts each account is reported. The same standard applies to every government, company and lab.

Sections

Top stories; ongoing issues; cyber categories (outages, attacks and campaigns, breaches, leaks, ransomware and unconfirmed claims, supply chain, vulnerabilities, law enforcement and policy); Microsoft and UK focus views; patch watch; coming up; global tech (geopolitics, AI, physical and infrastructure attacks, state spying and surveillance, major tech news); a claim tracker; and a list of sources swept, including any that could not be read.

Ticker

The bar at the top shows major developments and is refreshed hourly on working days.

Limits

The process can miss stories, particularly very recent social-media reports and sources that block automated reading. Treat unconfirmed items as claims. Errors are corrected with a dated update.
